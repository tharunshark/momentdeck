Open the studio
01 · Submit a link

Use a supported stream or video source, and confirm you have the right to process it.

02 · Review candidates

Check each suggested clip's score, source time, and duration before deciding what to keep.

03 · Download and post

Download the MP4 and publish it yourself when you are ready. The app does not auto-publish.

Prototype limits: MomentDeck runs locally. It currently uses the inherited source-fetching pipeline, does not analyze chat velocity or emotes, and outputs up to 1080p. Use only content you own or are authorized to process.
