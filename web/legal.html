Local prototype notice: MomentDeck is a self-hosted software project, not a hosted service. Its default setup is for one person on one computer.

Content and permission

Only submit a stream or video you own or have permission to process. You are responsible for following the source platform's terms and the creator's rules. A checkbox records your confirmation; the software cannot verify your rights.

How processing works

Source media and generated clips are kept in the local data folder. The inherited source pipeline uses third-party media retrieval software. If you configure an AI provider, transcript excerpts may be sent to that provider for highlight selection. Review that provider's privacy terms before enabling it.

Review and publishing

MomentDeck prepares clips for your review and download. It does not publish clips to YouTube or other social accounts. You decide whether, where, and when to post a downloaded clip.

Limitations

Highlight suggestions may miss context or be inaccurate. Review every clip before use. This prototype does not currently analyze live chat velocity or emote spikes, and its output is limited by the source pipeline rather than guaranteeing 4K.
